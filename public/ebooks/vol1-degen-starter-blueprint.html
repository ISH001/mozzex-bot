The On-Chain Landscape & DEX Mechanics

Most retail traders enter crypto through centralized exchanges (CEXs) like Binance, Coinbase, or Kraken. They deposit fiat, click buy on Bitcoin or Ethereum, and wait weeks for a 5% move. When they discover decentralized memecoins where tokens do 10x, 50x, or 100x in hours, they jump in blindly — and lose everything within 48 hours.

To succeed on-chain, you must understand the mechanical difference between a Centralized Exchange and a Decentralized Exchange (DEX):

Factor Centralized Exchange (CEX) On-Chain DEX (Solana / Raydium)
Custody Exchange holds your private keys You hold 100% self-custody in your wallet
Listing Speed Weeks/months of vetting Instant. Anyone can create a token in 30 seconds
Execution Central order book matching bids/asks Automated Market Makers (AMM) via Liquidity Pools
Price Volatility Low to Moderate (±3% to ±10% daily) Extreme (±50% to 1,000%+ in minutes)
💡 The AMM Constant Product Formula (x * y = k) When you buy a token on Raydium or Meteora, you aren't buying from a seller waiting with an order. You are depositing SOL into a liquidity pool and extracting token supply. The fewer tokens left in the pool, the exponentially higher each subsequent token costs. This is why small market cap tokens pump violently when volume arrives — and collapse just as violently when liquidity is drained.

Wallet Setup & Security Hardening

Your wallet is your bank vault. In on-chain trading, if you sign a malicious transaction, no customer support can reverse it. The blockchain is permanent.

The Two-Wallet Rule (Vault vs. Trench Burner)

Never trade directly from a wallet holding your life savings or long-term investments. You must operate with a strict two-wallet architecture:

  • Wallet 1: The Cold Vault: Stored on a hardware device (Ledger) or a clean browser profile. This wallet NEVER interacts with new token contracts, unknown dApps, or Telegram bots. It only sends SOL to your burner wallet and receives settled profits.
  • Wallet 2: The Trench Burner Wallet: The only wallet connected to DEXs and trading bots. You only fund this wallet with the exact trading capital you plan to risk for the week. If a contract is compromised, your maximum loss is strictly limited to the burner balance.
⚠️ The Seed Phrase Rule of Steel Never screenshot your 12 or 24-word recovery phrase. Never type it into Discord, Telegram, or any "support desk" website. Founders Claxy & Mozzari will NEVER ask for your seed phrase. Anyone asking for private keys is 100% an impersonator attempting to drain your funds.

Bankroll Management & Position Sizing Rules

Amateur traders think trading is about picking winning coins. Professional desks know that trading is 90% bankroll preservation. Even if you have a 70% win rate, poor sizing will liquidate you during a 3-trade losing streak.

The 2% Capital Allocation Model

Never allocate more than 2% to 5% of your total trading bankroll into any single trade. Here is how professional allocation works:

Total Bankroll Max Position Size (2% - 5%) Allowed Open Trades Stop-Loss Risk Per Trade
$100 USD $5.00 3 trades ($15 max exposure) -$1.50 (30% stop-loss)
$500 USD $15.00 – $25.00 4 trades ($80 max exposure) -$5.00 – -$7.50
$1,000 USD $30.00 – $50.00 5 trades ($200 max exposure) -$9.00 – -$15.00
🛡️ The Mozzex Golden Sizing Rule If losing a single trade makes you emotional or angry, your position size was too big. Sizing down allows you to execute trades with robotic discipline, following the plan without panic selling at the bottom.

Executing Your First Trade: Slippage & Gas

When you click swap on Solana, two critical variables determine whether your transaction confirms or fails:

1. Slippage Tolerance

Slippage is the maximum price change you allow between submitting your transaction and its confirmation on the blockchain. In high-speed memecoin launches, price can jump 15% in two seconds.

  • Normal Market Conditions: Set slippage between 1.5% and 3.0%.
  • High-Speed Trench Launches: Set slippage between 5.0% and 10.0%.
  • Danger Zone: Never set slippage to 50%+ or "Unlimited" unless you want MEV bots to sandwich your order and steal your profit.

2. Priority Fees (Gas Bribes)

Solana processes thousands of transactions per second. During high network congestion, validators prioritize transactions paying higher compute unit priority fees. Set a modest priority fee (0.001 - 0.005 SOL) to ensure your transactions never get dropped.
